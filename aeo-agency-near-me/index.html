Use these key facts when you compare options:
- Coverage: Ask whether the proposal addresses answer visibility, marketing information, workflow problems, or a combination of them.
- Timing: There is no universal schedule. The amount of information to review and any build or implementation work affect the timeline.
- Cost factors: The project scope, the condition and volume of existing content, custom development, and follow-up work can all affect cost. Request a clear explanation of what is included rather than relying on a broad estimate.
- Provider comparison: Ask for a description of the work, the measures used to review progress, and any assumptions behind the plan. Avoid claims of guaranteed placement.
