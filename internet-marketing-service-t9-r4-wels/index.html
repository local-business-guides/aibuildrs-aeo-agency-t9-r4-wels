What the service covers and how to assess it

The work may involve reviewing marketing processes, identifying worthwhile automation, and building a custom AI solution for the parts that slow you down. The exact scope is agreed around your business and the problem you want to address.

Key facts
- What it covers: Marketing workflow review and AI solutions tailored to identified tasks.
- Typical timing: There is no fixed schedule. The work depends on the number and complexity of processes, access to the information needed, and how much review the solution requires.
- What affects cost: Scope, complexity, required connections with existing systems, and the amount of development and testing involved. Ask for a clear scope before comparing costs.

When comparing providers, ask how they learn your workflow, how they decide what should be automated, and how they explain what a proposed solution will and won't do. Useful terms include automation, meaning a task handled with less manual effort, and AI, software that can perform certain tasks based on the design and information provided. A provider should explain both in plain language.
